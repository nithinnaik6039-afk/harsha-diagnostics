SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Friction in Modern Diagnostic Testing

[KEY CHALLENGES]
1. Patient Inconvenience:
   - Long waiting times and traffic queues in crowded diagnostic centers during early fasting hours.
   - High risk of infection exposure for senior citizens, pediatric patients, and pregnant women.
2. Field Phlebotomy Blindspots:
   - Zero real-time visibility into phlebotomist location, arrival estimates, or sample condition.
   - Biological sample degradation due to improper cold-chain maintenance during transit.
3. Administrative Overheads:
   - Manual phone dispatch, lost paper prescriptions, and delayed report delivery.

[TALKING POINTS / SPEAKER NOTES]
- Fasting blood draws require patients to travel early in the morning without food.
- Samples transported without strict temperature controls lead to pre-analytical errors and repeated punctures.
- Harsha Diagnostics solves these core friction points with automation and clinical-grade mobile workflows.

SLIDE 4: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

High-Performance Real-Time Architecture

[ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS]
- Client Layer     : 2 Expo React Native Apps + 1 React 19 / Vite Admin Dashboard.
- API Gateway      : Node.js / Express 5 REST API with JWT Auth & Role-Based Access.
- Live Telemetry   : Socket.io bidirectional event bus for continuous GPS broadcasting.
- Database Layer   : MongoDB with 9 Mongoose Schemas & Geospatial 2dsphere indexing.
- Cloud & Payments : Razorpay Payment Gateway, Expo Push Notification Server, NABL Cloud Vault.

[DATA PIPELINE]
1. Patient books test -> 2. Proximity-based MLT auto-dispatch -> 3. Phlebotomist navigates with GPS -> 4. Barcoded sample collected with cold-chain lock -> 5. Lab processes vial -> 6. Smart Report delivered to patient app.

SLIDE 8: CLINICAL QUALITY & NABL COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clinical Safety, Quality Assurance & Data Privacy

[SAFETY STANDARDS]
- NABL 112 Compliance: Full traceability of sample collection from puncture to report.
- Barcode Chain of Custody: Eliminates sample mix-ups through 1-to-1 vial QR binding.
- Biomedical Waste Audit: Strict sharps container and biohazard bag verification protocols.
- Phone Number Masking: Phlebotomists contact patients via virtual numbers to safeguard patient privacy.
- End-to-End Encryption: TLS 1.3 encryption for all patient health records and telemetry data.
